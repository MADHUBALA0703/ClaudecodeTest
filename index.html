        Rules for analytics

        • Analytics use counts and percentages only. Titles, descriptions and email text are never used.
        • Staff names are masked as initials by default. Showing them is a deliberate click, hides again after 60 seconds or when you leave the KPIs page, and is not remembered.
        • The Add Task form blocks text that looks like a card number, national ID, email address or password.
        • Nothing is saved in the browser. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything.

        Before using real data

        This is a training demo. A production version would need these controls first.

        • Sign-in and roles. Anyone with the page can see and change everything. Limit who can see names and descriptions.
        • Audit trail. Moves and deletions are not recorded against a person. Add a server-side log.
        • Approved email route. New-task emails go to an external service (FormSubmit). Replace it with an internal, approved channel.
        • Classification and retention. Agree what may be entered, who can see it and how long it is kept.
